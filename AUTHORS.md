Samir Baladi is the author of the Aether family of preprints, including
AetherGit, AetherFlow, AetherProof, AetherMesh, AetherGovern, AetherRuntime,
AetherOrchestrate, and the present candidate, AetherIdentity.

The Aether family investigates functional dimensions of coordinated agentic
software engineering. Each preprint adopts a falsification-first methodology:
failure conditions are stated before the candidate is described.
